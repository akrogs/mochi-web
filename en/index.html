The virtual pet with no ads

Mochi is a 3D pet for little ones. Care for it, cuddle it and talk to it, with no purchases and no internet needed.

Try Mochi before anyone else

Made for little ones

  • No ads, no purchases

    Nothing interrupts the game and nothing asks your child for money.

  • It listens and talks back

    It understands simple phrases in English and Spanish. Speech is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

  • Works offline

    Once installed, it plays just the same at home or on a trip.

  • It never gets ill or dies

    If you don't visit for a few days, it greets you happily.

  • Almost no text

    Everything works with pictures and gestures, no reading needed.

  • A grown-ups' area

    Locked for little ones, with play time, care and words learned.
